Example 1: Support and resistance

Support is a price level where the price keeps bouncing up because buyers step in. Resistance is a level where the price keeps stalling because sellers step in. A clean break through either level can signal a bigger move, but breakouts often fail too.

Example 2: Moving average

A moving average is the average closing price over the last few candles (here, 9). It smooths out noise. Price staying mostly above a rising average suggests a short-term uptrend; mostly below a falling one suggests a downtrend.
